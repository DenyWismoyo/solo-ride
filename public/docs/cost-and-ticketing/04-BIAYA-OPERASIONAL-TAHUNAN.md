## KOMPONEN OPEX RIDE-SOLO

```
1. INFRASTRUKTUR CLOUD (Firebase + Google Maps + Vercel)
2. SDM OPERASIONAL (tim inti yang menjalankan ekosistem)
3. SUPPORT & CUSTOMER CARE (driver + merchant + warga + OPD)
4. PERTUMBUHAN & MARKETING
5. PEMELIHARAAN TEKNIS (update, monitoring, security)
6. PROGRAM KOPERASI (subsidi karcis, asuransi driver)
7. CADANGAN & KONTINGENSI
```
